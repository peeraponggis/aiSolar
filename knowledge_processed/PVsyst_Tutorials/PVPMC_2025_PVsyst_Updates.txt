Updated guidelines for partial shading 1/2
• PVsyst shading models:
– Detailed IV curve evaluation (not discussed here)
– Faster "partition" approximation for regular PV 
rows
• Partition divides PV surfaces in "partitions" and 
apply a shading factor, according to partial shading
• See also e.g.
Hobbs, Anderson, Mikofski, Ghiz, "An approach to modeling linear and non-linear self-shading losses with pvlib", 2024, PVPMC
Martínez-Moreno, Muñoz, Lorenzo, "Experimental model to estimate shading losses on PV arrays", 2010, Sol. Energy Mater. Sol. Cells
Oliosi, Bridel, Mermoud, Wittmer, "Modeling Electrical Shading Effects in PVsyst", 2022, PVPMC
Oliosi, Mermoud, Wittmer, "Analysis of Electrical Shading Effects in PV Systems", 2021, PVSEC
